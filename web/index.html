05 · What to do

Recommendations for the product team

Ranked by value and effort. The first two share one root cause and one screen: users discover the real price at the last moment.

  1. Show the all-in price on the cart screen with a nudge: “Add €3.20 more to skip the small-order fee”. Fee shock · Medium effort
  2. Validate promo codes in the cart and show min-spend progress: “€4.20 more to unlock 20% off”. Failed promos · Low effort
  3. Turn on Apple Pay / Google Pay for mobile web, with inline 3-D Secure. Payment friction · Medium effort
  4. Show the delivery estimate on the store page; offer scheduled delivery late at night. Long ETAs · Low effort
  5. Guest checkout for new web users, creating the account after the order. Forced sign-up · Medium effort

Prove it: A/B test for fix #1

Primary metric
Cart → order conversion. It catches exits that just move to an earlier screen.
Secondary
Order Review exit rate, AOV, share of baskets just above €15
Guardrails
Fee revenue per order, Cart → Checkout Start rate, support contacts

How this was built

  1. DataPython generator: 185K sessions, 8-step funnel, fees, promos, ETAs
  2. SQLSQLite schema, an analysis view and 15 queries (CTEs, window functions)
  3. Pythonpandas, statsmodels: χ² and z-tests, logistic regression, power analysis
  4. Power BIStar schema, 34 DAX measures, field parameters, what-if slider
  5. WebThis page: static HTML + D3, data exported from the pipeline

Limitations: the data is synthetic and the analysis is observational. Sizing is in GMV, not profit; multiply by take rate for net revenue. The A/B test is how causality gets proven.
